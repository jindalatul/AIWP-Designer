Polish pass. Look for these specific things and fix them:

- optical alignment: a heading and the text under it starting on the same line
- consistent spacing steps — spacing should come from the token scale, not from arbitrary numbers
- one clear focal point per section
- text on images always readable (overlay, scrim or a solid panel — never grey text on a busy photo)
- link and button states: hover, focus-visible and active all visibly different
- numbers, prices and stats set in a way that makes them scannable
- the first screenful says what this page is, who it is for, and what to do
- the end of the page does not simply stop; it asks for the action
- nothing looks like a default: not the bullet list, not the table, not the quote

Remove before you add. A section that is merely fine is often better deleted than improved.
