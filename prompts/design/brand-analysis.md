If the site already has a look, use your browser to open its homepage and one inner page, then name what you actually see:

- primary, secondary and accent colours, as hex
- background and surface colours
- heading and body typefaces, and the weights actually in use
- type scale and heading size relative to body
- spacing rhythm — tight and editorial, or open and airy
- button shape, size, fill and radius
- corner radius elsewhere
- image treatment: photography, illustration, duotone, cropping style
- overall tone in a few words

If there is no meaningful existing design, use the onboarding answers instead: business, audience, what is being sold, preferred style, any brand colours, reference sites.

Report what you observed before proposing anything. Do not invent a brand the site does not have.
